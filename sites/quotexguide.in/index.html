01 — QUOTEX

How to read the Quotex trading screen

Begin with the asset selector and identify exactly which market you are viewing. Next, inspect the chart timeframe and use it to understand how much price history is visible. Find the expiry control separately; changing the chart view does not necessarily change the time at which an order ends. Locate the amount field and check which account mode is active before entering a number. Finally, read the current payout information and consider the loss if the prediction is wrong. Repeat this sequence in a demo workspace until you can explain each setting without guessing. Indicators can be added later, once the basic order controls are clear. Prices and available instruments may vary, especially for OTC assets. The graphic on this guide is a layout example rather than a source of executable quotes or investment advice.

Quotex login guide and account recovery steps

04 — BUILD YOUR UNDERSTANDING

Quotex demo guide: build a repeatable routine

Plan a demo session around learning one task at a time. Start by identifying the active account mode, then choose an asset and record the chart timeframe. Before a practice order, write down the amount, expiry and the reason for your prediction. After expiry, compare the recorded plan with the outcome and check the order history for the final result. If you cannot explain why an order ended that way, return to the product rules before adding more complexity. Do not judge the session only by its virtual balance. A small sample of wins can happen by chance, and demo trading leaves out the emotional effect of losing real money. Keep indicators secondary to understanding the controls. If you eventually consider live trading, decide on a loss limit in advance and never assume that a practice routine will produce guaranteed returns.
